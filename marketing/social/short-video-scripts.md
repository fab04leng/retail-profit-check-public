# 10 short-video scripts (Reels · Facebook · Xiaohongshu)

Each script is 20–40 seconds, vertical 9:16, with burned-in captions (most people watch on mute). Screen footage comes from `marketing/video/` or a fresh screen recording of the app on **demo data**. Keep the **"Demo data · fictional shop" / "示范资料 · 虚构店铺"** label on screen whenever a screen or a number from the demo shop is shown. Read [`../BRIEF.md`](../BRIEF.md) first.

**Who's on camera:** scripts marked 🎥 use the accountant on camera (name and face only with their consent). If they're not ready, use the "no face" alternative for each.

**Calls to action:** on Facebook/Instagram, "WhatsApp us, link in bio". On Xiaohongshu, use "私信我们" only, with no number, link or WhatsApp mention.
