# Content brief — Retail Profit Check social posts

The content generator reads this file every week, together with `brand-rules.md`. Edit the **"This week's notes"** section whenever you like; everything else can stay.

## Goal

Get Malaysian retail shop owners to **message us on WhatsApp** for a free 15-minute chat or a 5-minute demo on a fictional sample shop, so we can find our **first paid pilots (RM300 one-off)**. Success is conversations with real owners, not likes.

## Audience

- Owners and managers of **mall shops, minimarts, lifestyle and gift stores, pharmacies, hotel retail** in Malaysia, usually 1–5 outlets.
- Busy and practical. They read posts late at night after closing, on the phone, often in Chinese.
- They export sales from a POS (for example StoreHub, Loyverse, Qashier) and keep supplier invoices in an accounting system or in folders. Mention POS names only as examples of "your POS export", never as partners.
- Their worries: thin margins, rent, slow stock, supplier price increases, cash before festive seasons.

## The three pitches (rotated by the workflow)

Each week has three posting slots (default Mon / Wed / Fri, 9 pm). The workflow assigns one pitch per slot and rotates the order every week, so each pitch gets each day in turn. Every pitch is posted in English and casual Malaysian Chinese.

### 1. Profit by product (`profit_by_product`, code PROFIT)
- **Hook:** "Sales are good, but which products actually make money?"
- **Points:** profit by product and category; items that may sell below cost; items under the target margin (default 25%); promotions that quietly lose money.
- **Story ideas:** a demo-shop product that sells a lot but at a loss (fictional); "your best seller may not be your best earner".

### 2. Cash stuck in stock (`cash_in_stock`, code STOCK)
- **Hook:** "Some of your cash is sitting on the shelf."
- **Points:** stock value by age since last sale; possible dead stock (no sale in 90+ days); days of stock cover before re-ordering; planning festive orders.
- **Story ideas:** the back-room carton nobody remembers; ordering for the next festival without knowing what is still on the shelf.

### 3. Supplier invoice mistakes (`supplier_invoice`, code INVOICE)
- **Hook:** "Same supplier, same amount, two invoice numbers."
- **Points:** possible duplicate invoices; unit-price increases above 5%; invoices that may have been paid twice or overpaid. These are **honest mistakes** in busy shops. Never suggest dishonesty.
- **Story ideas:** a price that crept up 8% across three deliveries (demo data); two invoices for the same delivery a week apart (demo data).

## Formats to rotate

- **Slot 1: a recognisable situation** ("Year-end stock-take is coming…") and one question for the owner.
- **Slot 2: how it works.** Three short steps: export your files → we run plain rules → the accountant reviews every finding. Suggest a demo screenshot.
- **Slot 3: a myth or a question** ("Do you know your margin on your top 10 items?"), or answer a common objection (data privacy, "I already have an accountant": the accountant reviews our findings, we don't replace them).

## Seasonal hooks (use when relevant)

- **Nov–Dec:** year-end sales, stock-take, Deepavali leftovers, ordering for Chinese New Year.
- **Jan–Feb:** Chinese New Year stock, post-festive slow stock, new supplier price lists.
- **Mar–Apr:** Ramadan and Hari Raya ordering, cash before the festive peak.
- **May–Jun:** mid-year review, school holidays.
- **Jul–Sep:** Merdeka sales, quieter months for clearing slow stock.
- **Oct:** Deepavali, year-end planning.

## Things to avoid

Everything in `brand-rules.md`, especially: no "fraud", no "replaces your accountant", no tax advice, no guarantees, no invented numbers or testimonials. Any number must be labelled as demo data from the fictional shop.
