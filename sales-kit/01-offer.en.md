# Retail Profit Check — accountant-reviewed check of your shop's numbers

*For owners of shopping-mall shops, minimarts, lifestyle stores and hotel retail in Malaysia.*

## The problem

Most retail owners we speak to want the same thing: **reduce the loss, increase the profit.** But the answers are usually buried in exports nobody has time to go through:

- Some products may be selling **below cost or at a thin margin**, and nobody notices until year-end.
- Cash is **sitting on the shelf** as slow or dead stock.
- **Duplicate or wrong supplier invoices** can slip through — often a few thousand ringgit — and are usually found only at audit, if at all.
- It is hard to see **which weeks cash may run short** before it happens.

## What you get

We run your own sales, stock and supplier files through a set of checks, and a qualified (ACCA) accountant reviews every finding before you see it.

| Check | What it shows you |
|---|---|
| Profit | Profit by product and category; products sold below cost; products under your target margin (default 25%) |
| Stock | Stock value by age since last sale; possible dead stock (no sale in 90+ days); days of stock cover |
| Supplier invoices | Possible duplicate invoices; supplier unit-price increases above 5%; invoices that may have been paid twice or overpaid; payments with no matching invoice |
| Cash flow | An 8-week outlook that flags weeks where cash **may** go negative (an estimate, not a forecast you can bank on) |
| Health check | A one-page summary in RM. Every number links back to the rows it came from, so you can check it yourself |

The checks are **plain-English rules written with an accountant**. You can read them and change the thresholds. There is no AI making guesses: every finding comes with the rows behind it, and anything unclear is marked "needs review".
