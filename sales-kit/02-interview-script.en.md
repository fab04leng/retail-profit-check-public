# Owner interview script (15 minutes)

**Goal:** learn what really happens in the owner's business today, not whether they "like the idea". We want facts about **past behaviour**: what happened, what they did, and what it cost them.

**Rules for the interviewer**
- Ask about **the last time** something happened, not "would you…?" or "do you think…?".
- **Don't pitch** until minute 13. If they ask what we do, say: "I'll show you at the end, first I want to understand how you run things."
- Don't lead. Ask "How do you check margins?", not "Do you find margins hard to track?".
- Compliments ("Wah, good idea!") are **not data**. Steer back: "Thanks. When did this last happen to you?"
- Listen 80%, talk 20%. Silence is fine.
- Two people if possible: one asks, one takes notes.

---

## 0:00–2:00 Warm-up

- "Thanks for the time. This is not a sales call. We're trying to understand how retail owners in Penang handle their numbers. Can I take notes?"
- "Tell me a bit about the shop. How many outlets? How long have you been running it?"
- "Who does what: you, staff, bookkeeper, accountant?"

## 2:00–5:00 Margins and pricing

- "Tell me about the last time you changed a selling price. What made you do it?"
- "How do you know which products make money and which don't?"
- "When did you last find a product was making a loss or thin margin? How did you find out?"
- "What did you do about it?"

## 5:00–8:00 Stock and cash flow

- "Think of the last stock count or stock-take. What surprised you?"
- "Is there stock you know isn't moving? Roughly how much is sitting there?" (Write down their number; don't correct it.)
- "Tell me about the last month cash was tight. What happened? How did you handle it: delay suppliers, top up personally, overdraft?"
- "How far ahead do you look at cash today? What do you use?"

## 8:00–11:00 Suppliers, bookkeeping, accountant

- "How are supplier invoices checked before payment today? Who does it?"
- "Has a wrong or duplicate invoice ever been paid? How did you find out? How much was it?"
- "What does your accountant or bookkeeper do for you each month? What do you pay them, roughly per month or per year?"
- "How many hours a month do you or your staff spend on keying in sales, invoices, reports?"
- "What have you tried to fix any of this: software, POS reports, Excel, a new bookkeeper? What happened?"

## 11:00–13:00 Priorities

- "Of everything we talked about, which one costs you the most today, in money or time?"
- "If that was sorted, what would you do differently?"
- "Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?"

## 13:00–15:00 Short show and the ask

*Only now describe the offer, in 30 seconds:*
> "We're testing a check where software goes through your sales, stock and supplier files and my brother, an ACCA accountant, reviews every finding. You get a one-page health check in RM, with the rows behind each number. The files stay on an encrypted laptop and get deleted after the pilot."

**Ask for a commitment, from strongest to weakest:**
1. **Paid pilot:** "We're doing 2 paid pilots at RM300 on real data. Would you be one? What would you need to see first?"
2. **Data:** "Could you export 3 months of sales and a stock list so we can see what's possible?"
3. **Time:** "Can we book 30 minutes next week to show you the demo properly?"
4. **Introduction:** "Who else do you know running a shop who has the same headache?"

Close: "Thank you, really useful. I'll WhatsApp you a short summary."

## What counts as real commitment

| Strong signal (count it) | Weak signal (don't count it) |
|---|---|
| Pays RM300 for the pilot, or agrees a date to pay | "Sounds good", "Interesting", "Let me know when it's ready" |
| Sends real export files | "I'll think about it" with no date |
| Books a follow-up meeting with a date and time | "My friend might be interested" (no name/number) |
| Introduces another owner by name and number | Hypothetical prices ("I'd pay maybe RM500") |
| Brings in the person who actually controls the money or books | Praise for the idea |

**After each interview:** fill in the template within 1 hour, send the thank-you message (see `03-whatsapp-outreach`), and update the tally: *interviews done / pilots agreed / data received / intros*. Target: 10 interviews, 2 paid pilots.
