# Demo script: Retail Profit Check (5 minutes)

**Before the meeting:** open the app in the browser, choose the owner's language with the selector at the top right (English / 中文 / Bahasa Melayu), turn off notifications, and have a printed `01-offer` ready. Laptop charged. No internet needed once the page is loaded.

**Say this first, and repeat it if numbers come up:**
> "Everything you'll see is from a **fictional** Penang minimart with 2 outlets. It's not a real shop, and the numbers are made up for the demo."

**Golden rules:** read numbers off the screen and don't memorise or round them up. Say "possible", "may" and "needs review", never "you are losing X". Pause after each page and ask the question.

---

### 0:00–0:30 Start page → click **"Load demo data"**
- **Say:** "Normally you'd drop in your own exports: sales, stock list, supplier invoices, payments if you have them. They are analysed right here in the browser on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no AI. Today we'll use the demo shop."
- **Click:** **Load demo data**.

### 0:30–1:10 **Health check** page
- **Say:** "This is the summary in ringgit: cash stuck in slow stock, sales below cost, possible duplicate invoices, possible overpayments, and the lowest cash week ahead. Every number links to the rows behind it." *Click **See evidence** under "Possible duplicate supplier invoices", then **Show source rows** on the first card — the invoice lines appear exactly as they are in the file, with their row numbers.*
- **Ask:** "If this were your shop, which number would you look at first?"

### 1:10–1:50 **Profit** page
- **Say:** "Profit by product and category. These are products sold **below cost**, and these are **under the 25% target margin**."
- **Ask:** "How do you find out today when a product's margin drops?"

### 1:50–2:30 **Stock** page
- **Say:** "Stock value by how long since the last sale. This group is **possible dead stock**, meaning no sale for more than 90 days. And this is days of stock cover."
- **Ask:** "When did you last clear slow stock? How did you decide what to clear?"

### 2:30–3:10 **Suppliers** page
- **Say:** "Here are **possible duplicate invoices**, supplier price increases above 5%, invoices that may have been paid twice, and payments with no matching invoice. These are flags to check, not proof. The accountant looks at each one."
- **Ask:** "Has a wrong or double invoice ever slipped through for you? How was it found?"

### 3:10–3:40 **Cash flow** page
- **Show:** the demo has already filled in example inputs (cash in bank, rent, salaries, utilities). One week is marked **Below minimum** in red.
- **Do:** change **Cash in bank today (RM)** to a bigger number and watch the red week disappear; change it back.
- **Say:** "An 8-week **estimate**: recent sales trend in, supplier invoices by due date and your fixed costs out. That week is where cash **may** go below zero, because a big supplier payment falls due. It's only as good as the inputs."
- **Ask:** "How far ahead do you plan cash today?"

### 3:40–4:10 **Rules** page
- **Say:** "Every check is a rule written in plain English, so an accountant — my brother is ACCA — can read it, question it and change it. Nothing is hidden, and nothing is AI."
- **Do:** on rule **PRF-03**, change the target margin from 25 to 30. The "Flagged … times" count on the card goes up straight away. Set it back with **Reset all to defaults**.
- **Ask:** "What margin target would make sense for your shop?"

### 4:10–4:35 **Accountant review mode** (switch in the top bar)
- **Say:** "Before an owner sees anything in a pilot, the accountant marks each finding *Confirmed*, *Not an issue* or *Needs info*. Software does the routine work, and a person checks it."
- **Do:** tick **Accountant review mode**, go to **Suppliers**, click **Confirmed** on one finding and type a short note.

### 4:35–5:00 **Report** page
- **Say:** "At the end you get this as a written report — page one is the owner summary, then every finding with its rows — and we go through it together." Point at the review line: the confirmed finding is counted separately from the unreviewed ones.
- **Do:** click **Print / save as PDF**, show the preview, then cancel.

---

### After the demo: transition to the pilot ask
1. **Check:** "What was most relevant to you? What wasn't?" (Listen, and note their words.)
2. **Bridge:** "The demo shop is made up. The only way to know if this finds anything useful in *your* shop is to run it on your own data."
3. **Ask:** "We're doing 2 paid pilots at **RM300**. You share 3–12 months of exports, we run the check, my brother reviews every finding, then a 45–60 minute meeting with a written report, in about 2 weeks. Your files stay on an encrypted laptop and get deleted after the pilot if you want. Would you like to be one of them?"
4. **If yes:** agree on which files, who exports them and by when, then send the draft agreement (lawyer-reviewed version only).
5. **If not yet:** "What would you need to see first?" Then use `04-objection-handling`. Ask for one introduction.
6. **Pricing, if asked:** "After the pilot, we're **testing** a monthly price, probably somewhere around RM300–RM800 depending on outlets. Nothing is fixed, and there's no obligation."

**Never say:** "It replaces your accountant", "It gives tax advice", "You will save RM X", or "AI found…".

*Note: this script assumes Fabian or Joanne is speaking. If the accountant runs the demo, change "my brother" to "I".*
