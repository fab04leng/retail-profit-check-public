# Objection handling

**Principles:** listen first, and agree with what is true. Never overclaim. An objection is information, so write it down in the interview notes. If the honest answer is "then this isn't for you", say so.

**Facts you can say every time:**
- The checks are **readable rules an ACCA accountant can check and change**, with thresholds the owner can change. **No AI runs on your data.**
- Every finding shows **the rows it came from**. Anything uncertain is marked **"possible" / "needs review"**.
- It is **not audited accounts, not tax advice, and not a replacement for the accountant.**
- We **can't promise** any saving.

---

### 1. "Too expensive" / "No budget"
- **Behind it:** they don't yet see a return, or they've paid for tools before that didn't help.
- **Response:** "Fair. The pilot is RM300 one-off, so you can judge it on your own numbers before any monthly fee. We're still testing monthly pricing, and if the pilot doesn't show you anything worth more than that, you shouldn't continue."
- **Ask back:** "What does one wrong supplier invoice or a shelf of dead stock usually cost you? Has that happened in the last year?"

### 2. "My accountant already does this"
- **Behind it:** loyalty, or an assumption that the year-end accounts cover it.
- **Response:** "Good, keep them. Most accountants do statutory accounts and tax. This is a monthly look at margin by product, stock age and supplier invoices, which usually isn't in that scope. We can share the findings with your accountant too."
- **Ask back:** "When did your accountant last tell you which products are below your target margin, or which invoices may be duplicates?"

### 3. Data privacy: "I don't want to share my numbers"
- **Behind it:** fear of competitors, tax exposure or leaks. It is a reasonable worry.
- **Response:** "Understood. In the demo nothing leaves your computer: it runs in the browser, with no upload. For the pilot, files stay on an encrypted laptop, only our team sees them, they're never shared or sold, and they're deleted when you ask, with written confirmation. You can remove customer names and phone numbers first, because we don't need them. We follow PDPA 2010."
- **Ask back:** "What would you need to feel comfortable: an agreement, removing certain columns first, or running it on your own laptop with us beside you?"

### 4. "I don't trust AI"
- **Behind it:** stories of AI making things up, or not wanting a black box.
- **Response:** "Same as us, and my brother the accountant feels the same. There's no AI deciding anything. Each check is a written rule, for example 'flag a product if margin is below 25%', and you can read and change it on the Rules page. Every number links to the rows behind it."
- **Ask back:** "Can I show you the Rules page? Which rule would you want to set differently for your shop?"

### 5. "No time"
- **Behind it:** they're genuinely busy, or it's not a priority yet.
- **Response:** "That's the point: we want to save time, not take it. From you we need the exports, which take maybe 30 minutes if your POS can export, plus one 45–60 minute results meeting."
- **Ask back:** "How many hours a month do you or your staff spend on keying-in and reports now?"

### 6. "My POS already has reports"
- **Behind it:** they have sales reports and assume that's enough.
- **Response:** "Good, then the export is easy. POS reports usually show sales. They don't usually match supplier invoices against payments, or tell you how much cash is stuck in stock with no sale for 90+ days. We use your POS export as input."
- **Ask back:** "Which POS report do you actually open each month? What do you decide from it?"

### 7. "My shop is too small"
- **Behind it:** they think it's for chains, or that the amounts are too small to matter.
- **Response:** "Possibly. If you have one outlet and few suppliers, the pilot might not find much. That's why it's a RM300 pilot first and not a contract."
- **Ask back:** "How many suppliers and products do you have, roughly? How do you check invoices now?"

### 8. "What if you find nothing?"
- **Behind it:** fear of paying for nothing.
- **Response:** "Then you'll know your margins, stock and invoices are in order, with the evidence to show it, and you shouldn't buy a monthly service. We won't invent findings. Anything we're not sure about is marked 'needs review'."
- **Ask back:** "Would a clean result be worth RM300 to you as a check? If not, what would be?"

### 9. "My data is messy / partly on paper"
- **Behind it:** embarrassment, or they think it's not usable.
- **Response:** "Very common. We can start with whatever exports exist, even just sales and a stock list. Missing parts are shown as 'needs info', not guessed."
- **Ask back:** "Which of these can you export today: sales, product list, supplier invoices, payments?"

### 10. "Why should I trust a new team?"
- **Behind it:** no track record yet. That's true, so don't dodge it.
- **Response:** "Fair, we're early and this is a pilot. That's why there's a written agreement, a small fee, and a qualified accountant reviewing every finding. You can stop any time."
- **Ask back:** "What would you need to see from us first, like the demo, the agreement or a talk with my brother?"

### 11. "Can you do it free first?"
- **Behind it:** testing commitment, or a low perceived value.
- **Response:** "The demo is free, and you can see it now on a fictional shop. The pilot is paid because real data takes the accountant's time, and paying helps us know it's genuinely useful."
- **Ask back:** "After seeing the demo, what would make RM300 clearly worth it for you?"
