# Pilot Agreement — Retail Profit Check

**Date:** `[date]`

## 1. Parties

1. **Provider:** `[legal name of provider entity / sole proprietor]`, `[registration no.]`, `[address]` ("**Provider**"), represented by `[Fabian / Joanne]`.
2. **Reviewing accountant:** `[name]`, `[professional qualification, e.g. ACCA member no.]`, acting `[as partner of / as subcontractor to]` the Provider ("**Reviewing Accountant**").
3. **Client:** `[business legal name]`, `[registration no.]`, `[address]` ("**Client**"), represented by `[name, position]`.

## 2. Purpose and nature

2.1 The pilot is a one-off, time-limited trial of an analysis service for retail businesses.
2.2 The service is an **analysis aid**. It is **not** an audit, review engagement or assurance of financial statements. It is **not** tax, legal or investment advice, and it does **not** replace the Client's own accountant, auditor or tax agent.
2.3 Findings are **possible issues for the Client's consideration**. They are reviewed by the Reviewing Accountant but are based only on the data the Client provides.

## 3. Scope

The Provider will analyse the data supplied under clause 6 and report on:
(a) profit by product and category, products sold below cost, and products under a target margin (default 25%, adjustable);
(b) stock value by age since last sale, possible dead stock (no sale for more than 90 days), and days of stock cover;
(c) possible duplicate supplier invoices, supplier unit-price increases above 5%, possible double or over-payments, and payments with no matching invoice;
(d) an 8-week cash-flow **estimate** based on figures the Client provides (opening balance, fixed costs);
(e) a one-page summary.
Thresholds may be changed at the Client's request. Anything outside (a)–(e) is out of scope.

## 4. Fee

4.1 Fee: **RM300** (three hundred ringgit), one-off, `[inclusive / exclusive of any applicable tax — lawyer/accountant to confirm]`.
4.2 Payment: `[100% on signing / on receipt of data]` by `[bank transfer / DuitNow]` to `[account]`.
4.3 Any ongoing monthly service after the pilot is **not** part of this agreement. Any price discussed during the pilot is indicative only and requires a separate written agreement.

## 5. Deliverables and timeline

| Step | Target timing |
|---|---|
| Client provides data (clause 6) | Day 0 |
| Provider runs the checks; Reviewing Accountant marks each finding *Confirmed* / *Not an issue* / *Needs info* | Within ~10 working days of complete data |
| Results meeting (45–60 minutes, in person or online) | ~Day 14, by mutual agreement |
| Written report (PDF or printed), with evidence references for each finding | At or within 3 working days after the meeting |

Timings are targets, not guarantees. They may move if data is incomplete or questions are pending with the Client.

## 6. Client data

6.1 **Data requested:** 3–12 months of (a) sales export, (b) product/stock list, (c) supplier invoices, (d) payment records (optional).
6.2 **Data minimisation:** the Client should remove customer names, phone numbers and other customer identifiers before sharing, as they are not needed. The Provider will not intentionally request them.
6.3 **Client warranty:** the Client confirms it is entitled to share the data for this purpose.

## 7. Data handling and personal data protection

7.1 **Purpose limitation:** data is used **only** to perform this pilot.
7.2 **Transfer:** by `[encrypted USB / password-protected file via agreed channel — specify]`. Passwords are sent separately.
7.3 **Storage:** data is kept only on `[number]` encrypted laptop(s) controlled by the Provider. It is **not** uploaded to any external analysis service, and **no AI service** is used to process it. Access is limited to the Provider's named team and the Reviewing Accountant.
7.4 **No sharing:** data is **not shared, sold or disclosed** to any third party, except where required by law.
7.5 **Retention and deletion:** data and working copies are deleted within `[14]` days of the Client's written request, or at the latest `[60]` days after the results meeting, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. The Provider will confirm deletion in writing. `[Lawyer to advise whether the Reviewing Accountant must retain working papers under professional rules, and for how long.]`
7.6 **PDPA:** to the extent any personal data is processed, the Provider will handle it in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (as amended) and its principles, including security, retention and data integrity, and will process it only on the Client's instructions. `[Lawyer to confirm roles (data user / data processor) and any notice or breach-notification wording required.]`
7.7 **Incidents:** if the Provider becomes aware of loss of or unauthorised access to Client data, it will inform the Client without undue delay `[within X hours/days]`.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only for this pilot. This survives termination for `[2]` years. The Provider will not name the Client or use its results in any case study without the Client's **prior written consent**, and only in anonymised form.

## 9. No guarantees

9.1 The Provider does not guarantee that any issue will be found, or any saving, profit or recovery achieved.
9.2 Cash-flow figures are estimates based on assumptions and Client inputs, not forecasts to be relied on for financing decisions.
9.3 The Client remains responsible for its own business, accounting, tax and payment decisions, and should verify findings before acting (for example, before contacting a supplier about a possible duplicate invoice).

## 10. Limitation of liability

10.1 To the extent permitted by law, the Provider's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fee paid (**RM300**).
10.2 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit.
10.3 Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law. `[Lawyer to review enforceability.]`

## 11. Intellectual property

The Client owns its data and may use the report for its internal purposes. The Provider retains all rights in its software, rules and methods.

## 12. Termination

12.1 Either party may end the pilot by written notice (email or WhatsApp is acceptable).
12.2 If the Provider ends it before delivering the report, the fee is refunded in full. If the Client ends it after providing data, `[refund policy: e.g. no refund / 50% refund — to decide]`.
12.3 On termination, clause 7.5 (deletion) applies, and clauses 7, 8, 9 and 10 survive.

Governing law: Malaysia. `[Jurisdiction / dispute process — lawyer to advise.]` This document is the whole agreement for the pilot, and changes must be in writing.
