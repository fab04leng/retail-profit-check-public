# Demo data answer key

> **Synthetic data.** "Pinangria Mart (fictional demo shop)" is invented. Supplier names are coined words, product names are generic descriptions, and there are no real businesses, people or personal data. Generated by `src/demo/generate.ts` with seed `20261101`; regenerate with `npm run demo:export`.

**Shop:** two outlets (Jelutong, Bayan Lepas), 252 SKUs, 30 suppliers, 2025-11-03 to 2026-11-01 (52 weeks).
**Files:** `demo/data/sales.csv` (84,070 lines), `products.csv` (252), `supplier-invoices.csv` (5,974 lines), `payments.csv` (701).
**Patterns:** busier Fri–Sun; year-end (mid–late Dec 2025), Chinese New Year (17 Feb 2026, busiest 2 weeks before), Ramadan and Hari Raya Aidilfitri (~20 Mar 2026), festive-only products that sell only in season, about 8% growth over the year, occasional 10% discounts and refunds.
**Demo cash-flow inputs** (filled in by "Load demo data"): opening balance RM 20,000.00; Rent (2 outlets) RM 7,800.00 on day 1; Salaries, EPF & SOCSO RM 9,600.00 on day 7; Utilities & internet RM 1,900.00 on day 15.

## 6. Cash-crunch week (1)

With the demo inputs above and the default method, the estimated closing balance for the week of **2026-11-16** is **RM -4,148.77**: large year-end hamper and gift-tin orders dated 20 Oct 2026 (30-day terms) fall due on 19 Nov, on top of RM 71,625.05 of supplier invoices due that week in total. All other weeks stay above zero (lowest other week: RM 3,339.51). This is an estimate based on the trend method, not a prediction.

## 7. Thin margins by design (22)

Staples such as rice, sugar, flour, cooking oil, eggs, condensed milk, malt drink, diapers and formula milk were priced at 7–17% gross margin, as is common in Malaysian minimarts. They are flagged by PRF-03 (margin below the 25% target) — useful to show that a "finding" can be a deliberate choice the owner marks "Not an issue".
